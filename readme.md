#BEFORE I GO

My first SOLO game project is done!!! (˶˃ ᵕ ˂˶)
A short narrative puzzle game made in 4 days for the GMTK Game Jam 2026 (theme: Countdown).
Someone is losing their memories. Fragments resurface as photographs: memorize each one before the countdown runs out, then piece it back together.

#How to play
Click through the dialogues, memorize the photo before the timer ends, then drag the scattered pieces back to their place. Mouse only, playable in the browser.

#Made with
- Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas and Vite
- Pixel art drawn in LibreSprite by me
- Music and sound composed in Ableton Live by me
- Font: Tiny5 (Google Fonts)
- LABS Assets for Ableton
